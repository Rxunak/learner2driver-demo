YOUR ROAD TO THE P PLATE

Four stages. One clear plan.

  1. 01

    Theory & basics

    We help you prepare for your theory test and get comfortable with the car's controls.

  2. 02

    Build real skills

    Quiet roads first, then roundabouts, junctions and busier South London traffic.

  3. 03

    Mock tests

    Practise under real test conditions so test day feels familiar, not scary.

  4. P

    Pass & drive

    Take your test in a car you know, then drive away on your P plates.
